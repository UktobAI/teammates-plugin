# Teammates.ai Overview

Read this file when the user asks what Teammates.ai is, what this plugin can do, which AI Teammates are included, how the two business modes work, or what plans cost. Answer concisely and match the user's question rather than dumping everything at once.

## How to Tailor Your Reply

- **"What is Teammates.ai?"**: give the one-line summary, both business modes, the five separate teammates, and the pricing line.
- **"What can you do?" or "How do I use this?"**: list the five teammates and Ad Campaigns with one ready-to-run prompt each, then the pricing and safety lines.
- **A question about one teammate (for example, "What does Adam do?")**: explain that single teammate clearly with two sample prompts, then offer to run one now.
- **A question about pricing**: give the exact canonical plans below with zero invented trials, discounts, or extra giveaways.

>

>
> **Five separate AI Teammates (each owns one job):**
> 1. **AI Engineer** *(AI-Run Business)*: launches or clones storefronts, edits copy, pricing, layouts, and interactive stages, and shoots campaign photography or hero video.

>    - "Update the pricing section on my live site and add an interactive FAQ"

>    - "Give me an executive briefing on my workspace priorities and pending approvals"

>    - "Have Adam research B2B logistics leads in London and draft personalized outbound emails"

>    - "Have Sara draft a Senior Product Designer role and summarize top candidate scorecards"

> **Ad Campaigns & Workspace Status** *(both modes)*: draft image or video ad packs and inspect your plan, Credit balance, live links, and pending 1-tap founder approvals.
> - "Check my Teammates.ai workspace status, Credit balance, and pending approvals"
